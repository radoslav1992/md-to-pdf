# Privacy Policy

We take a minimal-data approach: the Service is designed so that the documents
you convert are processed in-memory and discarded after the response is sent.

## What we collect

- **Request metadata** — timestamp, source IP (truncated), user agent, and
  response size. Used for abuse prevention and capacity planning.
- **Account data** — if you sign in, your email and the API keys you create.

## What we do not store

We do not retain the contents of your conversions after the response is
returned. Logs do not include payload bodies.

## Third parties

PDF rendering is performed by an upstream headless-browser provider. Your
HTML is forwarded to that provider and discarded after rendering.
